How to install

Choose your device.

  1. Tap "Download for Android"If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway. This is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded fileIf asked, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome or Files, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open 9Wings Event and allow notificationsThis lets the app remind you about anniversaries, birthdays and payments.
  4. Turn off battery limitsLong-press the app icon → App info → Battery → Unrestricted (Xiaomi/Redmi: No restrictions and turn on Autostart).

Updating: download again from this page and install over the old app. Your data stays on the phone.

  1. Open the web app in Safarisheranr.github.io/9wings-app
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow, at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen"9Wings now opens like a normal app and works offline.

On iPhone, alerts show when you open the app. Only the Android app can alert you while it's closed.

  1. Open the web app in Chrome or Edgesheranr.github.io/9wings-app
  2. Optional: install itClick the install icon at the right end of the address bar to open it in its own window.
  3. Get your dataMore → Backup to Google Sheet → enter the link and key → Restore from sheet.

On a computer? Scan to open on your phone.
